Who We Are

Masii Christian Chapel is a ministry of Christian Chapel Kenya, a non-denominational Christian fellowship founded on sound Biblical truth, modeled after the New Testament church in Acts 2. We aim to be a beacon of faith and fellowship, led by the Holy Spirit.

Our Vision

To be a relevant and dynamic church expanding God's kingdom in Kenya and beyond.

Our Mission

To know Christ and make Him known around the corner and across nations through the Word of God and the power of the Holy Spirit.

What We're Building

Our Goals

Bible Nurture

Providing quality pulpit ministry, small group Bible studies, and discipleship programs to nurture faith and doctrine.

Community

Building social networks and offering care to orphans, widows, and the less privileged.

Outreach and Missions

Spreading the Gospel through evangelistic campaigns and neighborhood ministries.

Worship

Fostering a worship lifestyle to glorify God in all we do, with dynamic and contemporary Sunday services.
